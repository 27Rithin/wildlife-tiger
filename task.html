Chapter One: The Hunter Awakens

Dawn in the forest

Every tiger wears a unique stripe pattern, like a living fingerprint. Silent, patient and incredibly powerful, it moves through the forest like a shadow with a heartbeat. A hunt that looks effortless is never rarely successful: only about 1 in 10 attempts ends in a catch. A tiger can eat up to 40 kg of meat in a single meal, and its roar carries 3 km (3 × 103 m). Its skin is striped too, not just its fur, and every drop of H2O at a river bank is a hunting ground.

Chapter Two: Eyes of Amber

Master of the night

A tiger’s eyes see six times better than ours in low light. White spots on the back of its ears act like fake eyes, warning rivals that someone is always watching.
